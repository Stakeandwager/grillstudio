# Product mission

GrillStudio helps people turn an intention — a thought, a feeling, a story — into moving images and sound that produce a
deliberate audience reaction. It is a filmmaking engine, not just a video editor.

## Principles

1. **Intention first.** Every plan and every evaluation is judged against what the filmmaker intends and what the audience
   should feel. A clean export is not a successful film.
2. **One story, many methods.** Live action, animation and hybrid are production modes over a single shared project model.
   Switching mode never means rebuilding the story.
3. **Explain, don't decree.** Every requirement or recommendation says why it exists and which source it came from.
   Alternatives are offered where creative answers differ.
4. **The filmmaker approves.** Nothing is produced from a plan the filmmaker hasn't explicitly signed off. Changing the
   plan revokes the approval.
5. **Memory, honestly described.** Corrections become stored, searchable lessons. That is retrieval, not model training.
   Training/fine-tuning is a separate, opt-in capability that requires explicit approval.
6. **Private by default.** Media stays on the user's machine unless they explicitly choose otherwise. No uploads of private
   media, likeness or voice to external services without authorization.
7. **Useful to a beginner, deep enough for a professional.**

## Who does what

- **The builder** (autonomous engineering agent) builds, tests, documents and records decisions.
- **The user** tests the product and judges whether it fulfils the mission, reporting problems in ordinary language.

## Definition of success

See section 14 of the build directive: a user can give an idea, story, screenplay, image or video, and the engine
understands the intention, analyses the material, finds references, plans production (live action or animation), helps
create material, evaluates it, accepts corrections, preserves the approved knowledge and applies it next time.
